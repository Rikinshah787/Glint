# Code of Conduct

Glint follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy
community.

## In short

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized content, trolling, or publishing others' private information.
- Respect privacy: never share someone's captured data, screenshots or personal details in issues or PRs.

The full text, including enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban), is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and applies to all project spaces:
issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official channels.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers privately through GitHub: open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Rikinshah787/Glint/security/advisories/new) titled
"Code of Conduct report", or contact a maintainer listed on the repository. All reports are reviewed promptly
and handled confidentially.
